Hardship Assistance Policy

Section 1: Eligibility
Borrowers showing repayment stress may be considered for hardship assistance if payment delays are recent and there is evidence of financial difficulty.

Section 2: High Risk Borrowers
High risk borrowers should be reviewed for possible hardship assistance before escalation to stronger collection action.

Section 3: Recommended Actions
Eligible borrowers may be offered payment plan discussion, temporary payment support, hardship screening, or referral to a specialist.

Section 4: Restrictions
The model must not be the only reason for adverse action. A human analyst should review borrower context before escalation.

Section 5: Customer Treatment
The organization should use fair, transparent, and respectful communication when contacting borrowers about payment difficulty.
