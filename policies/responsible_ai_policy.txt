Responsible AI Policy

Section 1: Explainability
Every AI-assisted risk decision should include a clear explanation of the main risk drivers.

Section 2: Grounding
AI-generated recommendations must be grounded in model output and relevant policy documents.

Section 3: Human Oversight
High risk or uncertain cases require manual review.

Section 4: Fairness
Sensitive attributes should not be used as the sole basis for risk decisions. Model monitoring should include performance review and fairness checks.

Section 5: Hallucination Control
If there is no supporting policy evidence, the AI assistant should state that manual review is required.

Section 6: Decision Support
The system is a decision-support tool. It must not be used as the sole basis for lending, credit, or collections decisions.
