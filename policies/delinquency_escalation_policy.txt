Delinquency Escalation Policy

Section 1: Low Risk
Low risk borrowers remain in standard monitoring.

Section 2: Medium Risk
Medium risk borrowers receive payment reminders and continued monitoring.

Section 3: High Risk
High risk borrowers are escalated to the collections review team for early intervention.

Section 4: Very High Risk
Very high risk borrowers may require manual review, payment plan evaluation, and hardship assistance screening.

Section 5: Recommended Action Mapping
LOW risk maps to standard monitoring.
MEDIUM risk maps to payment reminder and monitoring.
HIGH risk maps to manual review and early intervention.

Section 6: Documentation
All recommendations should include reason codes, risk score, risk band, and policy references.
