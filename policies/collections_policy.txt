Collections Policy

Section 1: Early Intervention
Borrowers classified as high risk should be reviewed before severe delinquency occurs. Early intervention may include reminder communication, payment plan discussion, or hardship review.

Section 2: Medium Risk Accounts
Medium risk borrowers should receive a payment reminder and should be monitored for continued repayment deterioration.

Section 3: High Risk Accounts
High risk borrowers with repeated repayment delays, high utilization, low payment-to-balance behavior, or severe repayment delay history should be referred to the collections review team.

Section 4: Low Risk Accounts
Low risk borrowers should remain under standard portfolio monitoring.

Section 5: Human Review
Automated model outputs must be used only as decision-support. Final collections actions require human review when the borrower is classified as high risk.

Section 6: Documentation
Every recommended collections action should include the customer identifier, risk band, default probability, key risk drivers, and recommended next step.
